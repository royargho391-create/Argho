A few quick answers for anyone starting out.

Is Argho for beginners?

Yes. Argho is designed to welcome beginners and help them find a clear first step.

Will I earn money immediately?

No website or course can honestly guarantee that. Results depend on your skills, practice, portfolio, and ability to find clients.

What tools will I need?

You can start with a phone or computer and free learning tools. Some advanced software or services may cost money.
